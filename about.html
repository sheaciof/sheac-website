About Us

Self Help Environment Awareness Camp

Self Help Environment Awareness Camp (SHEAC) is a youth-led environmental and biodiversity conservation organization based in Pokhara, Nepal. SHEAC brings together young people, students, researchers, conservationists, and communities to promote environmental awareness, biodiversity conservation, research, education, and meaningful action.

We believe that lasting environmental change begins with informed and empowered people. Through research, education, youth engagement, community initiatives, and conservation activities, SHEAC works to connect knowledge with practical action for nature and society.

Our Vision

A future where biodiversity thrives, ecosystems are valued, and communities are empowered to live in harmony with nature.

Our Mission

To promote biodiversity conservation and environmental responsibility through research, education, youth leadership, awareness, and community-based conservation action.

Our Theme

Think Globally, Act Locally

SHEAC believes that global environmental challenges require local understanding and local action. Our theme reflects our commitment to connecting global environmental responsibility with practical conservation efforts within our communities.

What We Focus On

Thematic Areas

Biodiversity & Wildlife Conservation

Promoting the conservation of wildlife, plants, habitats, and biological diversity.

Environmental Awareness & Education

Raising environmental awareness and encouraging environmentally responsible communities.

Research

Supporting scientific research and evidence-based approaches to environmental and conservation challenges.

Youth Engagement & Leadership

Creating opportunities for young people to participate, learn, lead, and contribute to conservation.

Community-Based Conservation

Working with communities to develop practical and locally relevant environmental solutions.

Plant & Ecosystem Conservation

Promoting the protection of native plants, forests, ecosystems, and their ecological functions.
