About SHEAC

Youth-led action for biodiversity and a healthier environment

Who We Are

Empowering Youth for Environmental Action

Self Help Environment Awareness Camp (SHEAC-NEPAL-IOF) is a youth-led environmental and biodiversity conservation organization based in Pokhara, Nepal. Operating at the Institute of Forestry, we combine research, education, community engagement, and conservation action to address pressing environmental challenges.

Vision

A future where biodiversity thrives and communities actively participate in protecting nature.

Mission

To conserve biodiversity and promote environmentally responsible communities through research, education, youth leadership, and community-based conservation action.

Core Values

Conservation

Protecting biodiversity, fragile ecosystems, and wildlife habitats.

Science

Using research and evidence-based methods to address environmental issues.

Youth Leadership

Empowering students and young advocates to become effective leaders.

Community

Partnering directly with local communities for sustainable impact.

Action

Translating environmental awareness into practical on-the-ground solutions.
