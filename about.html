About SHEAC

Youth-led action for biodiversity and a healthier environment

Who We Are

Self Help Environment Awareness Camp (SHEAC) is a youth-led environmental and biodiversity conservation organization based in Pokhara, Nepal. We combine research, education, community engagement, and conservation action to address environmental challenges.

Vision

A future where biodiversity thrives and communities actively participate in protecting nature.

Mission

To conserve biodiversity and promote environmentally responsible communities through research, education, youth leadership, and community-based conservation action.

Core Values

Conservation

Protecting biodiversity and ecosystems.

Science

Using research and evidence to understand environmental challenges.

Youth Leadership

Empowering young people to become conservation leaders.

Community

Working with communities as partners in conservation.

Action

Turning environmental knowledge into practical solutions.
