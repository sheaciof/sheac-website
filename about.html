Who We Are

Self Help Environment Awareness Camp (SHEAC) is a youth-led environmental and biodiversity conservation organization based in Pokhara, Nepal.

SHEAC brings together young people, students, researchers, conservationists, and communities to promote environmental awareness, biodiversity conservation, research, education, and meaningful action.

We believe that lasting environmental change begins with informed and empowered people. Through research, education, youth engagement, community initiatives, and conservation activities, SHEAC works to connect knowledge with practical action for nature and society.

OUR DIRECTION

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To promote biodiversity conservation and environmental responsibility through research, education, youth leadership, awareness, and community-based conservation action.

Our Vision

A future where biodiversity thrives, ecosystems are valued, and communities are empowered to live in harmony with nature.

OUR THEME

Think Globally, Act Locally

Think Globally, Act Locally

Environmental challenges are global, but meaningful conservation action begins with understanding and responding to local realities. SHEAC encourages young people and communities to understand wider environmental challenges while taking practical action within their own communities and ecosystems.

WHAT WE DO

Our Thematic Areas

Our work connects conservation, research, education, youth leadership, and community action.

Biodiversity & Wildlife Conservation

Supporting awareness, research, and conservation initiatives focused on wildlife, birds, plants, habitats, and biodiversity.

Environmental Awareness & Education

Promoting environmental knowledge through awareness programs, educational activities, outreach, and community engagement.

Research

Encouraging student and community-based research that contributes to better understanding of biodiversity, ecosystems, and environmental challenges.

Youth Engagement & Leadership

Creating opportunities for young people to develop leadership, conservation skills, and meaningful environmental participation.

Community-Based Conservation

Working with local communities and stakeholders to encourage practical conservation solutions and environmental responsibility.

Plant & Ecosystem Conservation

Supporting the conservation of plants, forests, habitats, and ecosystems that sustain biodiversity and human well-being.
