Who We Are

Bridging Youth Energy with Conservation Science

Self Help Environment Awareness Camp (SHEAC) is an active, youth-driven environmental organization based in Pokhara, Kaski, Nepal. Founded by forestry students and conservation enthusiasts, SHEAC connects scientific field research, ecological education, biodiversity monitoring, and grassroots youth participation.

Operating under our steadfast ethos, "Think Globally, Act Locally", we empower local communities, students, and young researchers to take proactive ownership of natural ecosystems.

Who We Are

Self Help Environment Awareness Camp (SHEAC) is a youth-led environmental and biodiversity conservation organization based in Pokhara, Kaski, Nepal. Founded by forestry students and enthusiastic conservationists at the Institute of Forestry (Pokhara Campus), SHEAC acts as a pivotal platform connecting field research, ecological awareness, wildlife preservation, and community empowerment.

Our Mission

To inspire, educate, and mobilize youth and local communities toward active biodiversity conservation, scientific ecological research, and sustainable environmental stewardship through local community engagement and global knowledge sharing.

Our Vision

An environmentally conscious society where youth lead biodiversity conservation efforts, enabling peaceful human-wildlife coexistence and resilient ecosystem health across Nepal.
