Activities

Field activities, awareness camps, and events organized by SHEAC.

The activities below are oragnized or collaborated by SHEAC in Pokhara, Nepal.
Environmental Education

Tree Identification Program

Institute of Forestry, Pokhara & Nearby Areas

SHEAC regularly conducts tree identification programs around the Institute of Forestry, Pokhara, and nearby areas to help students develop practical knowledge of local tree species. The program provides opportunities for students to learn tree identification through direct observation and field-based learning.

Training & Capacity Building

Proposal Writing Workshop

Institute of Forestry, Pokhara

SHEAC conducts an annual Proposal Writing Workshop to help students build practical knowledge and skills in proposal development. The workshop introduces students to the key components of a research or project proposal and helps them develop the skills needed to structure and prepare effective proposals.

Training & Field Activity

Butterfly Identification Training

Institute of Forestry, Pokhara

A butterfly identification training program providing students with theoretical and field-based knowledge on butterfly identification and observation. More than 30 students participated in the training.

Training & Capacity Building

IEE/EIA Skills Development Training

Institute of Forestry, Pokhara Campus, Pokhara, Kaski

SHEAC conducted an IEE/EIA training program for undergraduate students, providing an opportunity to develop their understanding and practical skills related to Initial Environmental Examination (IEE) and Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA). More than 35 students participated in the training.

Wildlife Training

Camera Trap Handling Training

Institute of Forestry, Pokhara Campus, Pokhara, Kaski

On the occasion of Wildlife Week, SHEAC collaborated with CLCC to conduct a training program on camera trap handling, providing participants with practical knowledge and skills in the use and handling of camera traps for wildlife-related activities.

Cleanliness Program

Cleanliness Program

Institute of Forestry, Pokhara Campus, Pokhara, Kaski

On the occasion of Wildlife Week, SHEAC collaborated with the Institute of Forestry (IOF), Pokhara Campus, and other green organizations to organize a clean-up program around the IOF premises, promoting environmental cleanliness, awareness, and collective action for a cleaner and greener campus.

Community Awareness

Forest Fire Community Awareness Program

Pokhara Metropolitan City, Ward No. 23

SHEAC conducted a community awareness program on forest fire prevention and management in Pokhara Metropolitan City Ward No. 23 in a coloboration with SAFE-Nepal and Ward no.23.` More than 25 community members and community forest representatives participated in the program.

Collaborative Conservation Initiative

March for Marbled Cat

Lakeside, Pokhara

SHEAC collaborated with other organizations of the Institute of Forestry to organize the “March for Marbled Cat” conservation initiative in Lakeside, Pokhara. The initiative included a conservation rally and hike aimed at spreading awareness among local people and visitors about the conservation of the Marbled Cat and the importance of protecting its habitat.

Environmental Education

School Awareness Program

Sikles, Kaski

SHEAC conducted a school awareness program for Grade 9 and 10 students in Sikles, focusing on proper waste management and its connection with human–wildlife interactions. The program highlighted how unmanaged waste can attract wildlife toward human settlements and discussed responsible waste management practices that can support safer human–wildlife coexistence.

Community Awareness

Community Awareness on Waste Management

Sikles, Madi Rural Municipality, Ward No. 1

SHEAC conducted a community awareness program in Sikles focusing on local waste management practices and the importance of proper waste management for human–wildlife coexistence. The program engaged community members in discussions on responsible waste management and how better waste practices can contribute to safer coexistence between local communities and wildlife. The program was supported by the Annapurna Conservation Area Project (ACAP) and Madi Rural Municipality Ward No. 1 Office.

Wildlife Awareness

Snake Awareness Program

Institute of Forestry, Pokhara

SHEAC conducted a snake awareness program for students at the Institute of Forestry, Pokhara. The program focused on identifying different types of snakes, understanding the differences between venomous and non-venomous snakes, and learning appropriate snake handling techniques. The activity aimed to improve students' knowledge and awareness of snakes and promote safe and responsible interactions with them.
