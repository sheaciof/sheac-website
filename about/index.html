About SHEAC

About Us

Self Help Environment Awareness Camp (SHEAC) is committed to environmental awareness, biodiversity conservation, research, and youth-led local action.

Who We Are

Self Help Environment Awareness Camp

स्वंयसेवी वातावरण जागरण समूह

Self Help Environment Awareness Camp (SHEAC) is a youth-led environmental and biodiversity conservation organization based in Pokhara, Kaski, Nepal.

SHEAC brings together young people, students, researchers, conservation enthusiasts, and community members who share an interest in protecting nature and strengthening environmental awareness.

Through research, environmental education, biodiversity activities, community engagement, and local initiatives, SHEAC aims to encourage practical conservation action.

Our work follows a simple guiding principle:

Think Globally, Act Locally

Our Mission

Turning Awareness Into Local Action

To encourage young people and communities to understand, value, and actively contribute to the conservation of biodiversity and the environment.

SHEAC seeks to support practical conservation through environmental awareness, field activities, research, education, and community participation.

Our Vision

A Conservation-Minded and Environmentally Responsible Society

We envision a society where young people, communities, researchers, and institutions work together to protect biodiversity and build a sustainable relationship with nature.

Our long-term vision is grounded in knowledge, participation, responsibility, and locally meaningful conservation action.

Our Theme

Think Globally, Act Locally

Environmental challenges can be global, but meaningful conservation also begins with action within our own communities.

SHEAC uses this principle to encourage students and young people to understand wider environmental issues while taking practical action at the local level.

What We Work On

Thematic Areas

Our activities can connect several areas of environmental and biodiversity conservation.

Biodiversity Conservation

Supporting awareness, field activities, biodiversity documentation, and conservation initiatives.

Wildlife Conservation

Encouraging wildlife research, conservation education, and responsible coexistence between people and wildlife.

Bird Conservation

Supporting bird observation, monitoring, research, awareness, and conservation activities.

Plant Conservation

Promoting plant identification, environmental education, ecological understanding, and plant conservation.

Butterfly Research

Encouraging butterfly observation, documentation, research, and awareness of insect biodiversity.

Environmental Awareness

Organizing awareness activities, youth engagement, environmental education, and community initiatives.

Research & Documentation

Supporting student research, field studies, scientific documentation, and knowledge sharing.

Youth & Community Engagement

Connecting young people and local communities with environmental and biodiversity conservation activities.

Our Approach

From Learning to Action

SHEAC encourages practical environmental engagement through a simple cycle of learning, participation, documentation, and action.

01

Learn

Build knowledge about biodiversity, ecosystems, wildlife, plants, and environmental issues.

02

Explore

Take knowledge into the field through observation, surveys, training, and practical activities.

03

Document

Record observations, research findings, experiences, and environmental information.

04

Act

Turn knowledge into practical conservation, awareness, and community action.
