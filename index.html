Who We Are

Turning environmental knowledge into practical conservation action

Self Help Environment Awareness Camp (SHEAC) is a youth-led environmental organization working to conserve biodiversity, promote environmental awareness, and encourage community participation in nature conservation. We connect scientific research, youth leadership, and local community outreach.
