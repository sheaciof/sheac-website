Who We Are

Working Together for Nature

SHEAC brings together young people, students, researchers, educators and communities to contribute to environmental conservation and biodiversity protection.

Think Globally, Act Locally

Self Help Environment Awareness Camp (SHEAC) is a youth-led organization committed to environmental awareness, biodiversity conservation, research, education and community engagement.

We believe that meaningful conservation begins with local understanding and collective action. By connecting young people with communities, researchers and conservation initiatives, SHEAC works to turn environmental knowledge into practical action.

What We Do

Our Main Areas of Work

Our activities connect environmental knowledge, biodiversity conservation, research and community participation.

Environmental Conservation

Promoting practical actions that support healthy ecosystems, natural resources and sustainable environments.

Biodiversity & Wildlife

Supporting awareness, research and conservation of wildlife, birds, plants and biodiversity.

Research

Encouraging student and community-based research that contributes to environmental understanding.

Environmental Education

Creating opportunities for learning, awareness and meaningful environmental engagement.

Youth Engagement

Empowering young people to participate in conservation, leadership and environmental initiatives.

Community Initiatives

Working with communities and partners to develop locally relevant environmental solutions.
