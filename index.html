Who We Are

Self Help Environment Awareness Camp (SHEAC) is a youth-led environmental and biodiversity conservation organization based in Pokhara, Kaski, Nepal. Founded on the principle of local empowerment, we bring young people, researchers, and communities together to protect ecosystems and foster environmental stewardship.

Our work spans across rigorous biodiversity conservation, youth-driven environmental awareness campaigns, ecological research, and grassroots action aimed at building climate-resilient landscapes across Nepal.

Main Areas of Work

Biodiversity Conservation

Protecting vulnerable wildlife habitats, flora, and critical ecosystems in Pokhara and surrounding regions through community stewardship.

Research

Conducting baseline biodiversity studies, ecological monitoring, and environmental assessments to support data-backed decision-making.

Environmental Awareness

Educating communities and schools through interactive workshops, awareness rallies, and ecological campaigns.

Youth Engagement

Empowering the next generation with practical leadership skills, field experience, and environmental advocacy tools.
