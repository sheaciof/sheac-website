Our Core Pillars

Integrating conservation science, field advocacy, and youth-led waste innovation.

Wildlife Conservation

Research and advocacy targeting vulnerable focal species in Nepal, including the snow leopard, pangolin, swamp deer, and lesser adjutant.

Green Loop Pokhara

Pioneering circular waste management, upcycling workshops, and "Best Out of Waste" initiatives across local schools and communities.

Community Leadership

Facilitating focal group discussions, youth oratory platforms, and educational campaigns to mitigate human-wildlife conflict.

Featured Flagship Initiative

Green Loop Pokhara

Transforming plastic and biodegradable waste into community assets through hands-on training, school engagement, and public advocacy.
