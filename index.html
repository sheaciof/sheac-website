Who We Are

Bridging Youth Energy with Conservation Science

Self Help Environment Awareness Camp (SHEAC) is an active, youth-driven environmental organization based in Pokhara, Kaski, Nepal. Founded by forestry students and conservation enthusiasts, SHEAC connects scientific field research, ecological education, biodiversity monitoring, and grassroots youth participation.

Operating under our steadfast ethos, "Think Globally, Act Locally", we empower local communities, students, and young researchers to take proactive ownership of natural ecosystems.

Core Pillars

Our Thematic Focus Areas

Dedicated pathways driving ecological stability and environmental education across Nepal.

Biodiversity Conservation

Protecting fragile ecosystem habitats and supporting landscape-level conservation actions in Western Nepal.

Wildlife Conservation

Promoting wildlife protection, camera-trapping surveys, public awareness, and human-wildlife coexistence models.

Bird Conservation

Conducting avian fauna surveys, monitoring migratory waterfowl across Pokhara's lake clusters, and birding education.

Plant & Butterfly Studies

Plant identification drives, butterfly biodiversity mapping, herbarium documentation, and urban forestry initiatives.

Environmental Awareness

Organizing school awareness camps, eco-club workshops, plastic-reduction drives, and public rally campaigns.

Research & Education

Encouraging field research, mentorship for undergraduate thesis students, and publishing open scientific insights.
