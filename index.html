Empowering Youth, Protecting Nature

Self Help Environment Awareness Camp (SHEAC) is a youth-led organization working for environmental awareness, biodiversity conservation, research, and community engagement.

Learn More Get Involved
Who We Are

Working Together for Nature

SHEAC brings together young people who are passionate about environmental protection, biodiversity, conservation research, and community action.

Environmental Awareness

Promoting environmental knowledge and responsible action through awareness programs and community engagement.

Biodiversity Conservation

Supporting conservation of wildlife, plants, habitats, and ecosystems through practical conservation initiatives.

Research

Encouraging youth-led research and scientific approaches to understand environmental and biodiversity challenges.

Youth Engagement

Creating opportunities for young people to learn, participate, lead, and contribute to conservation.

Think Globally, Act Locally

Be part of a generation working for a healthier environment and a future where people and nature thrive together.
