Self Help Environment Awareness Camp (SHEAC) is a youth-led environmental and biodiversity conservation organization based in Pokhara, Nepal, combining research, education, and community engagement.

Working Together for Nature

Youth working for environmental protection, biodiversity conservation, research, education, and community action.

Who We Are

SHEAC brings together young people who are passionate about environmental protection, biodiversity conservation, research, education, and community action.

We believe meaningful conservation begins with knowledge, collaboration, and action at the local level.

Learn More About Us

Think Globally, Act Locally

Our activities connect local communities and young people with practical conservation, environmental awareness, biodiversity research, and meaningful environmental action.

We work to create opportunities for young people to learn, participate, lead, and contribute to conservation.
