Field photographs · Bara District, Nepal

As a collaborating organization in NAFAN's Biodiversity Monitoring Programme, the Self Help Environment Awareness Camp (SHEAC) contributed to biodiversity field assessments across four collaborative forests of Bara District, Nepal.

The field initiative focused on exploring and documenting different components of forest biodiversity, including:

  • Tree diversity and forest composition
  • Bird species and their presence
  • Wildlife and habitat observations
  • Species richness, abundance and ecological insights

These observations contribute to a better understanding of forest ecosystems and can support biodiversity conservation and forest management. SHEAC is grateful to NAFAN for the collaboration and opportunity to contribute to documenting Nepal's natural heritage.
